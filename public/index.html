How We Build & Ship

Made for Daily Use

Simple, well-built hardware without fragile plastic shells or locked-down software.

01 — Solid Materials

Heavyweight metal that lasts

We use machined 6063 aluminum, stainless steel, magnesium alloy, and Grade 5 titanium. Our keyboards stay planted on your desk, our watch cases take real hits, and our mice stay rigid without creaking.

02 — Easy to Customize

No bloated driver software

Our keyboards and number pads use standard hot-swappable switch sockets and VIA browser keymapping. You can change shortcuts right in your browser and swap switches or keycaps without soldering.

03 — Fast LA Shipping

Packed and shipped by us

We stock everything in-house at our Los Angeles shop. When you place an order, we check it, pack it in foam, and hand it off to USPS, UPS, or FedEx within 1–2 business days.
