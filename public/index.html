Design & Manufacturing Philosophy

Engineered Without Shortcuts

Built in small production runs for industrial designers, software engineers, and tactile hardware enthusiasts.

01 — Monoblock Metallurgy

Milled from Solid Billet, Never Cast

Every Monolith enclosure begins as a solid bar of 6061-T6 aircraft aluminum, Grade 5 titanium, or C36000 brass. Five-axis CNC milling achieves sub-0.02mm tolerances, crisp diamond-chamfered edges, and acoustic density that molded plastic cannot replicate.

02 — Open Firmware & Serviceability

Designed to Be Owned for Decades

Our keyboards and macro pads feature 5-pin hot-swappable switch sockets, QMK and VIA browser keymapping, standard stainless Torx fasteners, and pure virgin PTFE skates—making routine maintenance and switch customization effortless.

03 — Direct Los Angeles Fulfillment

Inspected, Foam-Packed & Tracked

We fulfill every order directly from our Los Angeles warehouse at 6515 S Crenshaw Blvd. Each unit undergoes a multi-point QC check, is packed in custom high-density protective foam, and ships via USPS, UPS, or FedEx within 24–48 hours.
